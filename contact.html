Hi, I’m Sultan — a polyglot and the founder & developer behind Sultan Academy, an independent learning ecosystem built to break down traditional financial and rigid barriers in online education.

What began as a personal system of strict discipline and smart time management has grown into a global, zero-commission platform uniting languages, coding, music, and practical skills under one roof.

Unlike conventional platforms that lock people into single boxes and take heavy cuts from every lesson, I engineered Sultan Academy so anyone can both learn and teach from one account — supported by direct peer connections, original study guides, and live community rooms.

Knowledge shouldn't be gated by middlemen. Whether you're here to master a language, build software, play an instrument, or find your next project partner — welcome home.

Zero middlemen. Endless skills. Let's build, learn, and grow together!
